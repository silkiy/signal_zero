Status: **done**, 6 November 2026 (Asia/Jakarta). Reviewed by root and CLI agent.

`SimulationRunner` executes the production tick engine, emits immutable snapshots including tick zero, and caps storage at 65 snapshots. Identity/versions are bound to the puzzle.
