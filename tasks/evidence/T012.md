## Intermediate slice boundary

This task intentionally does **not** apply SHIFT (T013) or evaluate success/collision/synchronization/failure (T014). A movement candidate may therefore still be `RUNNING` at a future terminal point in this intermediate slice; a subsequent attempt to advance beyond the budget is rejected. Tests assert candidate movement fields, not a misleading temporary `RUNNING` requirement at those points. No full replay runner or playable-game claim is made before the dependent engine tasks.

`EngineState`'s public typed constructor and `copyWith` are internal domain restore APIs for the future solver's compact state arena, not JSON/backup ingestion. They deep-freeze inputs; callers preserve semantic status/reason/puzzle constraints. External state decoding needs its own validated boundary. `toJson()` emits only `tick`, `signals`, `switches`, `used_edges`, `target_arrivals`, `status`, `reason`; puzzle hash and diagnostic IDs remain separate getters.
